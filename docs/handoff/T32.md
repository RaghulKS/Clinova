## What works
- **Results screen in demo-video order.** Funnel and risk share the first row; then come "Where to look first" (flags), "What happened next" (the reveal, for NCT loads) and "Suggested fixes". Secondary funnel charts sit in an expander. The footer reads "Clinova advises; the protocol author decides." It appears on every tab.
- **0:20–1:10.**
  - Loading an NCT ID shows the chip "Protocol as originally registered on 25 Jul 2017", using the API's `registered_on`.
  - The funnel is a protocol-order waterfall: remaining and dropped per criterion, then eligible, would enroll, would finish. The flagged criterion is highlighted, with a callout giving its counts. That is the top flag; with no flags, it is the largest single-rule exclusion.
  - KPI tiles: screened, eligible, enroll, finish.
  - Risk cards show the probability, its range, confidence and drivers.
  - With real metrics, each card adds its percentile among held-out test trials (from `docs/predictions.csv`).
  - A target with no test metrics shows "No model yet" and no number (amendment in v1.0).
  - A target whose AUC interval is not above 0.5, or whose Brier score is not better than the baseline's, shows "Not validated" (shortfall in v1.0).
- **Flags.** Each flag shows where, why and what to do.
  - Dollar line: the expected value of the fix that changes the same rule. With no amendment model, it says the value is not estimated, and why.
  - When every flag has `delta_p` 0, the section says they are ranked by exclusion share, not by modeled risk.
- **1:10–1:30 reveal.**
  - The trial card shows the outcome and enrollment (1 of 27 planned for the demo trial).
  - A horizontal timeline comes from the trial record and `trial_versions`: first registered, study start, eligibility changes (accent), status change or termination (accent).
  - A strong callout appears when the sponsor later widened a rule Clinova pointed at (demo: I3 prior platinum, removed).
  - The rule-change table shows the first-registered source text. Each row is marked "Flagged by Clinova" or "Also changed by the 'Fewest dropouts' fix" (the cardiac exclusion).
  - When the version history is not loaded, the reveal says so instead of showing a date.
- **1:30–2:10 fixes.**
  - Three fix cards in columns: edits in plain words (symbols, units, lists), enrollment speed, dropout, diversity, amendment risk and expected value. Each shows its range, or "not estimated: no amendment model yet". Precedents appear when present.
  - Accept calls `/fixes/accept`. It shows a before/after table with a Change column (better in accent), the basis and the elapsed seconds.
  - Before/after has a new "Would enroll, share of screened" row (`Measures.enroll_speed`). This is the quantity the optimizer's `delta_enroll` refers to. Before, the optimizer estimate pushed "enroll rate" to 100%.
- **Tabs.**
  - Validation: AUC vs baseline with intervals; calibration with a target picker, zoomed to the data; hit rate and ablation empty states that point to the caveats. Each chart has one computed sentence, and the not-validated sentence comes from metrics.
  - Replay: the curve plus a computed "retrained better in N of M months" sentence.
  - Pulse: a clean "not run" state.
  - Audit log: counts, percent formatting, newest 500.
- **States.**
  - Empty: a dashed box everywhere, including an empty start screen with a how-it-works strip.
  - Loading: spinners and stage progress.
  - Errors: plain sentences. The API has a global handler that answers 500s with one sentence and logs the trace. The client flattens 422 validation lists and never shows a raw body.
  - "fixture data" is an amber tag. It no longer appears on a real trial's history.
- **API presentation changes (`clinova/api`):**
  - `demo_cache.py` serves `docs/demo_results/<NCT>.json` and `<NCT>_optimize.json` when the protocol run equals the snapshot (rules and visits; lock toggles ignored; fixes on locked rules dropped). Results are tagged `source: cache`, which saves the two-minute optimizer run. Any edit goes to the live engine.
  - `ValidationResponse.test_p`: sorted test-split p per target.
  - `Measures.enroll_speed`.
  - An engine that cannot load (`ImportError` / `OSError`: missing libomp or model bundle) falls back per stage with the usual fixture label instead of failing the run.
  - Placeholder risks (driver `no_model`) are never logged as predictions.
  - The optimizer-estimate accept path is tagged with the run's source instead of always "fixture".
  - `python -m clinova.api` re-execs itself with scikit-learn's libomp on a Mac where LightGBM cannot load.
- **Screenshots:** `docs/screens/01..11` at 1920×1080 on the real v1.0 outputs. `docs/screens/README.md` holds the review notes and the best five for the deck.

## Decisions
- **Risk level words.**
  - With test percentiles: High at the 80th percentile or above, "Above typical" from the 60th, else "Typical or lower".
  - Without them (fixture metrics): absolute bands at 40% and 20%.
  - The number and range always sit next to the word. The demo trial therefore shows "Typical or lower" for accrual. That matches the gate 2 finding that the model misses this trial; we do not dress it up as high.
- **"Validated" rule:** test AUC interval low above 0.5 **and** Brier better than the baseline's. It is computed only from `docs/metrics.json`, and only when the source is live.
- **Funnel highlight:** the top flag when there is one (the model's pick), else the largest single-rule exclusion.
- **Flag dollar figure:** borrowed from the fix that edits the same rule (`CriterionFlag` has no dollar field); "not estimated" when there is no amendment model.
- **Cached demo results win over the live engine for the exact snapshot:** same model version, and the optimizer takes about 2 minutes. The screen says so.
