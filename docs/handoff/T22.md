# T22 handoff: features, LightGBM, calibration, SHAP, flags, validation

Status: **partial**. Everything the task asks for is built and runs end to end on the live database. The amendment model, the hit rate and the simulation ablation have no numbers yet because their inputs (amendment labels, parsed amended versions, 300+ simulated trials) do not exist yet. The code computes them as soon as the data lands, and a background job retrains automatically.

## What works

- **`model/features.py`**: `build_features(trials, protocols, sim_features, *, embeddings, ref, cohort)`.
  - Leakage is blocked by construction: from a `trials` row it reads only `nct_id` and `first_version`. Design fields come from T11's own `ingest.pull.map_trial` applied to the first-version JSON alone.
  - Features:
    - rule counts (total, inclusion, exclusion, unparseable, share unparseable, distinct fields, comorbidity exclusions, driver mutation required, prior platinum required, prior IO excluded);
    - per-field allowed bounds from T12's `parse.diff` pass-set logic, turned into `strict_*` (positive = stricter than the training median) for: max ECOG, min CrCl, ANC, platelets, hemoglobin, max AST/ALT/bilirubin, min life expectancy, max/min prior lines, min PD-L1, brain-mets policy, min stage, age limits, number of histologies allowed;
    - eligibility text length;
    - visits (count, hours, invasive, travel), estimated with `parse.visits.estimate_visits` when no schedule is given;
    - design: phase, arms, randomized, masking, intervention types, log planned enrollment (first version, ESTIMATED type only);
    - operations: sites, countries, US share, industry or government sponsor;
    - **screen features**: eligible fraction and largest single-rule exclusion on a fixed 2,000-persona virtual cohort (seed 42, cached at `.data/model/cohort_any_2000_42.parquet`);
    - 16 PCA components of the eligibility embedding, fit on train only;
    - simulation features (mean over first-version `sim_runs` seeds; NaN when absent).
  - No start year. No Gemini calls anywhere in `clinova/model/`.
- **`model/train.py`**: `train(df, cfg) -> bundle`.
  - One LightGBM per label. Tuned over an 8-point grid with time-ordered validation inside train (fit before 2015, validate 2015 to 2017).
  - Isotonic calibration on 5-fold out-of-fold predictions.
  - 30 bootstrap models per label.
  - Logistic baseline on 10 interpretable features.
  - Targets with fewer than 5 labeled trials of either class are skipped and listed in `bundle["skipped"]`.
  - `save_bundle` / `load_bundle(version=None)` write and read `<main clone>/models/<version>/bundle.joblib` plus `meta.json`. That directory is gitignored and shared by all worktrees; `None` loads the latest. T21 can call `train(df, {"bootstraps": 0, "ref": ref})`.
- **`model/evaluate.py`**: `evaluate(bundle, df)`.
  - Test ROC AUC with a 1,000-resample bootstrap 95% CI for LightGBM and the baseline, Brier, and 10-bin reliability.
  - Hit rate against two baselines: 3 random rules (exact expectation) and the 3 largest marginal exclusions.
  - Simulation ablation with a paired bootstrap CI.
  - Writes `docs/metrics.json` (validates as `ValidationMetrics`) and `docs/figures/reliability.png`, `docs/figures/shap_importance_accrual_fail.png`, `docs/figures/shap_importance_shortfall.png` (SHAP TreeExplainer, mean |SHAP| on test).
- **`model/predict.py`**:
  - `score(protocol_or_trial, *, sim=None, model_version=None)` returns three RiskScores in target order.
    - p is the mean of the calibrated main and 30 bootstrap models. The range is the 2.5 to 97.5 percentile over bootstrap models times simulation seeds.
    - Each score carries the top 4 SHAP drivers in plain words and a confidence badge: mean cosine distance to the 10 nearest training embeddings, low above the training 90th percentile, high at or below the median, low when there is no embedding (pasted drafts).
    - A target without a model (now: amend) gets an explicit placeholder: p 0.5, range 0 to 1, low confidence, and the driver "No amend model yet ...: this 50% is a placeholder, not a prediction".
    - With no bundle on disk, it raises `NotImplementedError` so the API keeps its fixture fallback.
  - `flag_criteria(protocol, *, k=3)` relaxes each unlocked, parseable rule one step or removes it.
    - The relax ladder is the next looser threshold that other loaded NSCLC first versions actually used, or the next vocabulary step for stage and brain mets.
    - For each edit it re-screens the cached cohort, rebuilds the features and re-scores. Rules are ranked by the drop in p_accrual_fail, with ties broken by marginal exclusion.
    - Each flag carries where, why (exclusion share and the risk change, in numbers), what to do, `delta_p` and `suggested_edit`.
  - Timing on the demo trial: score about 4 s cold and 0.7 s warm; flags 0.6 s.
- **CLI**:
  - `python -m clinova.model train [--version V] [--bootstraps 30] [--use-sim auto|yes|no]`;
  - `python -m clinova.model evaluate [--version V]`;
  - `python -m clinova.model watch` (see background jobs).
- **Tests** (`tests/model/test_model.py`, offline, fixture cohort):
  - features from first-version JSON alone match features from the full row, and also match a row with later or label-side values swapped in;
  - design features equal `map_trial` of the same JSON;
  - rule bounds and strictness on the fixture protocol;
  - relax directions;
  - train, save, load, score and flags round trip, including the no-bundle fallback;
  - metric helpers;
  - hit-rate counting.
- `make check` equivalent (`uv run ruff check . && uv run pytest -q`): ruff clean, **238 passed**.

**Reading the numbers honestly.**
- On 186 unseen 2018+ trials, LightGBM ranks accrual failures somewhat better than chance (AUC 0.71). The interval overlaps the logistic baseline (0.68), so the gain is not established.
- Its calibration is poor. The Brier score of 0.197 is worse than the baseline's 0.134, and worse than a constant at the training prevalence. The reliability plot shows overprediction above p 0.5: with 21 positives in train, the isotonic map learned steps that do not transfer.
- For shortfall the model beats the baseline on both AUC (0.63 vs 0.56) and Brier (0.228 vs 0.270), but the intervals overlap.
- The embedding PCs and planned enrollment dominate the accrual SHAP ranking, and rule strictness and screen features contribute little. So `flag_criteria` deltas are tiny, and the ranking mostly falls back to marginal exclusion. Each flag's `why` text says so.
- The simulation earned no measurable accuracy here, because there was too little of it to test.
- The calibration choice (mean of the calibrated bootstraps rather than the single calibrated model) was made on the train-internal 2015 to 2017 validation only, never on test. Train-internal Brier for accrual: 0.161 for the bagged mean vs 0.183 for the single model, against 0.165 for a constant.

## Decisions

- **Simulation features** come from `sim_runs` directly (`features.sim_row`, mean over seeds), not from T24's `sim_features` view. Train and serve then share one function. The 300-trial gate counts distinct simulated NCT trials. Fallback applied: shipped without simulation features, ablation reported as not computable with counts.
- **Screen features** (eligible fraction and largest exclusion on a fixed virtual cohort) are protocol features available for every parsed trial at zero Gemini cost. They are what makes "re-screen, rebuild features, re-score" in `flag_criteria` meaningful. They are not counted as simulation features in the ablation.
- **Missing rule = NaN, not "no restriction"**, because 44% of rules are unparseable and a missing parsed rule does not prove the protocol has no such restriction.
- **Served p** is the mean of the calibrated main and bootstrap models. Ranges are bootstrap times seed percentiles, widened to include p.
- **Untrained targets** get a labelled placeholder RiskScore so the contract's three scores in order hold and the UI shows "placeholder" rather than silently dropping the target.
- **Relax ladders** use thresholds from all loaded first-version protocols (train and test). These are protocol text, not outcomes. Medians, PCA and kNN distances use train only.
- **Confidence:** high at or below the training median kNN distance, medium up to the 90th percentile, low above it, and low when there is no embedding.
- **Bundles** live in `<main clone>/models/` (gitignored) so the API on main and other worktrees see them. Proposed setting: `docs/contract_changes/T22.md` item 2.
