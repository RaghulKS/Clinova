# Handoff T03: NSCLC clinical trait distributions (spike)

## What works
- `docs/spikes/clinical_distributions.md`: sourced distributions, evidence labels, correlations, trial-eligibility sanity check, list of assumptions to revisit.
- `docs/spikes/assumptions.draft.yaml`: machine-readable, one key per trait, `source:`, `assumption:`, `evidence:` and `confidence:` on every entry. Parses with PyYAML; a one-off check confirmed every `probs` map and age band set sums to 1 and every flagged entry has a `source` key.

## Stubbed or assumed
- 16 of 42 flagged YAML entries are `assumption: true` (listed in the md). Largest gaps: ECOG marginal and its age gradient, baseline lab failure rates (no NSCLC data found), serum creatinine distribution, treated vs untreated brain metastases, never-smoker share, Hispanic share, squamous driver rates, PD-L1 band split.
- Several figures were seen only in search excerpts (marked `evidence: excerpt`); re-verify before quoting externally.

## How to use
Integrator: copy the YAML to `clinova/cohort/assumptions.yaml` (T13 consumes it). Sampling order: age, sex, race, Hispanic flag, smoking, histology, ECOG given age band, driver (one categorical, EGFR odds x5.5 for Asian, cap 0.65), PD-L1 for driver-negative, brain metastases given histology and age, prior lines, weight and SCr then Cockcroft-Gault, labs. Use `eligibility_sanity_check.recommended_band_for_simulator_output` (25-65%, central 30%) as a plausibility check only.

## Numbers produced
Key proposals: age median 69; male 53.6%; White/Black/Asian 77.3/9.3/2.7%; nonsquamous 70%; ECOG 0/1/2/3+ 20/45/27/8%; EGFR 46% never vs 5% ever (nonsquamous); PD-L1 <1/1-49/>=50 33/37/30%; brain metastases 14% nonsquamous, 5% squamous; model CrCl<60 in 21%; trial-eligible share 30% (Trial Pathfinder, 10 phase III trials, mean).

## Decisions
- Prefer US EHR/registry cohorts (Flatiron, LCMC1, BIDMC, NCDB, GENIE) over non-US; non-US data used only for direction or flagged.
- Used PubMed/PMC E-utilities text for primary reads where the web fetch was blocked.
- Weight from NHANES general population (no NSCLC weights found), with a `weight_multiplier` knob.
