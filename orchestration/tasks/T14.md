# T14 Persona agents, simulation runner, surrogate

Owns `clinova/simulate/agents.py`, `runner.py`, `surrogate.py`, `simulate/__main__.py`, and tables `sim_runs`, `persona_outcomes`. Read ARCHITECTURE.md 5.5, `docs/CONTRACTS.md` and the fixtures. T13 builds the cohort and screen in parallel on another laptop: use the fixture personas and a fixture screen until Gate 1 swaps them in.

1. **Agents.** Build a plain-language trial offer from a ParsedProtocol (purpose, randomization, each visit's day, procedures, hours, travel, total duration). `run_agents(personas, protocol, seed)` sends batches of 25 personas per call through `common.llm.generate_json` and returns validated AgentDecisions. Each persona block gives life context and a short clinical summary. Ask each person to decide as that person would, with a concrete reason and burden tags (travel, time off work, caregiving, procedure fear, side effects, cost, distrust, too unwell). Never give base rates or outcomes. Retry personas missing from a response. Async, batches concurrent.
2. **Runner.** `simulate_trial(protocol, n=1000, seeds=(1,2), max_agents=200)`: cohort → screen → sample up to max_agents eligible → agents → SimRunSummary (eligible_frac, enroll_rate, dropout curve by visit, median dropout visit, top reasons). Writes `sim_runs` and `persona_outcomes`.
3. **Behavior check (most important).** Three fixture protocols: light (6 visits, no biopsy), medium, heavy (20 visits, 2 mandatory biopsies, long infusions, weekly travel). Run live, 2 seeds each. Record in `docs/agent_sanity.md` whether enrollment falls and dropout rises with burden. If not, iterate on the prompt (persona specificity, reasoning before decision) up to three times and log each change.
4. **Surrogate.** `Surrogate` fit on `persona_outcomes` predicting enroll and dropout from visit-burden features and persona traits; `score(protocol, cohort_df) -> (enroll_rate, dropout_risk)` in under 20 ms. Refit later by T24.
5. **CLI.** `python -m clinova.simulate batch --n-trials N --seeds 2 --max-calls M --dry-run` prints planned calls and tokens before spending.

**Done when:** the behavior check is recorded, the runner writes real rows, the measured cost per simulated trial is in the handoff, and `make check` passes.
