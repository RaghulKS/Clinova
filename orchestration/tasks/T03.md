# T03 Spike: NSCLC clinical trait distributions

Research only. Write `docs/spikes/clinical_distributions.md` and `docs/spikes/assumptions.draft.yaml`. No Makefile yet: skip `make check`. Every number needs a cited, linked source (SEER, NCI, cohort studies, guideline papers). Never invent a number; if none is found, propose a value marked `assumption: true` with your reasoning.

For US patients with advanced or metastatic NSCLC who are candidates for systemic therapy: age; sex; race and ethnicity; stage; histology; ECOG 0/1/2/3+; EGFR, ALK, ROS1 positivity (with variation by ancestry and smoking); PD-L1 TPS bands; brain metastases (none, treated and stable, untreated); prior systemic lines by setting; creatinine clearance (or age-based Cockcroft-Gault inputs); lab abnormalities that commonly breach eligibility (ANC, platelets, hemoglobin, AST/ALT, bilirubin). Note key correlations (ECOG with age; EGFR with never-smoking and Asian ancestry; CrCl with age). Also find published estimates of the share of real NSCLC patients who meet typical trial eligibility; the simulator uses it as a sanity check.

The YAML must be machine-readable: one key per trait, distribution type and parameters, conditional tables where relevant, and `source:` on every entry.
