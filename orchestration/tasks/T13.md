# T13 Virtual cohort, code screen, diversity index

Owns `clinova/cohort/`, `clinova/simulate/screen.py`, `clinova/simulate/diversity.py`. Read ARCHITECTURE.md 5.3–5.4, `docs/CONTRACTS.md`, `docs/spikes/personas_mirofish.md`, `docs/spikes/clinical_distributions.md`, `docs/spikes/assumptions.draft.yaml`.

Fan out three parallel parts:

1. **Personas.** `scripts/get_personas.py` downloads the sample named in the spike to `DATA_DIR/personas/` (never commit data). `cohort/personas.py` maps fields to Persona and reweights to NSCLC demographics by raking or importance resampling; deterministic per seed. Fallback per the task header.
2. **Clinical traits.** Copy the spike YAML to `clinova/cohort/assumptions.yaml` and keep every `source:`. `cohort/traits.py` samples ClinicalTraits conditional on demographics and setting, with the documented correlations (ECOG with age; EGFR with ancestry and smoking; CrCl via Cockcroft-Gault from age, sex, weight, creatinine). `generate_cohort(n, seed, setting) -> list[Persona]` plus a dataframe form.
3. **Screen.** `simulate/screen.py` evaluates CriteriaRules on the cohort dataframe with numpy, skips and reports unparseable rules, returns ScreenResults and a Funnel (sequential stages + per-rule marginal exclusions). `apply_edits(rules, edits)` for the optimizer. Target: 10,000 personas × 25 rules under 50 ms, with a benchmark test. `simulate/diversity.py`: `diversity_index(eligible_df)` comparing age, sex and race/ethnicity mix to the NSCLC reference (1.0 = matches).

Tests: hypothesis property tests (relaxing a threshold never shrinks the eligible pool; no rules → everyone eligible) and a golden funnel on the fixture protocol. Write `docs/cohort_sanity.md` comparing the fixture protocol's eligible fraction with the published eligibility shares from the spike, honestly.

**Done when:** `generate_cohort` and `screen` work on real data paths, the benchmark passes, and `make check` passes.
