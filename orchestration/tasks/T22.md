# T22 Model and validation

Owns `clinova/model/`. Read ARCHITECTURE.md 5.6 and 7, `docs/gate1_report.md`, `docs/CONTRACTS.md`. **Goal:** calibrated, explained risk scores for accrual failure, shortfall and amendment, validated on unseen trials, every number honest.

Fan out parallel parts:

1. **Features** `model/features.py`, first-version data only: rule counts by kind; per-field strictness relative to the training median (max ECOG, min CrCl, brain-mets policy, max prior lines…); unparseable count; text length; visit count, hours, invasive procedures, travel; phase, arms, masking, allocation, intervention types, first-version enrollment target; sites, countries, US share, sponsor class; 16 PCA components of the embedding (fit on train only); simulation features from the `sim_features` view (NaN when absent). Exclude start year. A test rebuilds features from first-version JSON alone and matches.
2. **Training** `model/train.py`: `train(df, cfg) -> bundle`. One LightGBM per label, tuned with time-ordered CV inside train (fit < 2015, validate 2015–2017). Isotonic calibration on out-of-fold predictions. Logistic baseline on 10 interpretable features. 30 bootstrap models per label. Versioned bundles in `models/<version>/` (gitignored if large; record paths).
3. **Metrics** `model/evaluate.py` on the test split: ROC AUC with bootstrap 95% CI for LightGBM and baseline; Brier; 10-bin reliability plot; simulation ablation (with vs without sim features, simulated test subset, paired bootstrap CI). Writes `docs/metrics.json` and PNGs in `docs/figures/`.
4. **Explanations and flags.** SHAP TreeExplainer: global importance plot and per-trial top drivers with readable names. `predict.flag_criteria(protocol)` ranks rules by the drop in predicted accrual-failure probability when relaxed one step or removed (re-screen cached cohort, rebuild features, re-score). Each CriterionFlag says where, why, what to do. **Hit rate:** among test trials with `label_amend`, the share where a relaxed or removed field from `parse.diff.diff_rules` is in the top 3 flags, against 3 random rules and the 3 rules with the largest marginal exclusion.
5. **Confidence badge** from embedding distance to the 10 nearest training trials vs the training distribution (above the 90th percentile = low).

`predict.score(protocol_or_trial)` returns RiskScores whose ranges combine bootstrap and simulation-seed spread. Start immediately without simulation features; when `sim_features` has 300+ rows (check every 30 minutes; T24 fills it), retrain with them. Write the numbers and one honest paragraph reading them into the handoff.

**Done when:** `docs/metrics.json`, figures, `score`, `flag_criteria` and `train` exist, and `make check` passes.
