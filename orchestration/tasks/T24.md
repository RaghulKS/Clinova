# T24 Batch simulation and surrogate refit

Owns the batch path in `clinova/simulate/` (runner, surrogate, `__main__.py`) and the `sim_features` view. Start this task first in your wave: it is the longest job and T22 depends on its output.

1. Choose about 400 trials, stratified by split and by each label so positives are well represented; include every test-split trial with `label_amend`, the demo trial and both backups. Save `data/sim_sample.csv` (small, committed) and the sampling rule in the handoff.
2. Dry-run the cost. Keep total calls inside the remaining `MAX_GEMINI_CALLS` budget for this laptop's key, leaving 15% headroom for other tasks. Launch the batch with 2 seeds as a resumable background job. Run the demo trial and backups with 5 seeds each.
3. While it runs: create the `sim_features` view (one row per trial: eligible_frac, enroll_rate, dropout at visits 2, 4 and last, dropout-curve area, top burden-reason shares, seed spread). Keep a progress line in the handoff and retry failures at the end.
4. Once 300+ trials are done, refit the surrogate on all `persona_outcomes`, report held-out accuracy, and keep `Surrogate.score` under 20 ms.

**Done when:** at least 300 trials are simulated (or the fallback applied), `sim_features` exists, the surrogate is refit, and `make check` passes. Leave the batch running if it isn't finished.
